1. The Problem

Public Data Is Difficult

Government budgets and project reports contain important information, but they are often long, technical, and difficult for ordinary citizens to navigate.

AI Creates a New Risk

AI makes information easier to access, but a confident answer without evidence can still mislead people.

The Trust Gap

Citizens need information that is not only understandable, but also easy to independently verify.

“Public information shouldn't require a government insider to understand.”

2. The Solution

CivicPulse turns official Lagos public data into understandable, evidence-backed answers.

ASK

Ask natural-language questions about Lagos State budgets, spending, and public projects.

VERIFY

Match questions to structured records extracted from official Lagos State government publications.

ACT

See the source, reporting period, source page, and official document so you can check the information yourself.

AI explains evidence. It does not invent evidence.

5. What CivicPulse Can Do

Ask

“How much has Lagos spent on waterways?”

Returns verified expenditure for relevant waterways projects and supporting evidence.

Rank

“What are the largest Lagos State projects by 2026 budget?”

Ranks projects using verified 2026 budget records.

Clarify Instead of Guessing

“Tell me about Lagos budget.”

CivicPulse asks for more detail and offers useful questions instead of guessing what the user means.

6. The Answer Is Only Half the Product

What the User Gets

  • A direct answer in plain language.
  • Relevant project or budget information.
  • Performance data where available.
  • Reporting period.

What the User Can Verify

  • Official source title.
  • Publisher.
  • Source page.
  • Link to the official document.

Don't just trust the answer. Check the evidence.
