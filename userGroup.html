04 Beyond the Certification
A certification says...
“I have learned something.”
Impact says...
“I know how to create value with what I've learned.”

The real question isn't: How many certifications do you have?

What have you done with the knowledge those certifications gave you?

05 Turn Knowledge Into Value

Can you solve?

Can you solve a real problem, not just answer an exam question?

Can you build?

Can you build, automate, improve or simplify a process?

Can you teach?

Can you explain complexity clearly and help someone else grow?

Can you create value?

Can you help a business make better decisions and achieve better outcomes?

Can you create opportunity?

Can you open a door for someone who is where you once were?

Can you create impact?

Can your knowledge produce something people can actually use?

06 Don't Confuse Inspiration With Imitation
Learn from people
  • Copy their discipline.
  • Copy their consistency.
  • Copy their willingness to learn.
Then add your own
  • Your perspective.
  • Your experience.
  • Your solution.

The Salesforce ecosystem doesn't need another version of someone else.

It needs you.

07 Innovation Is Often a Better Question

Innovation doesn't always mean inventing something that has never existed.

“Can this be done better?”

Faster?

Easier?

Cheaper?

More accessible?

Sometimes innovation is simply seeing something familiar differently.

08 Africa Gives Us Problems Worth Solving
Our context matters

Our businesses.
Our communities.
Our realities.

Those realities create opportunities for people who are willing to build, experiment and improve.

  • Don't always wait for someone somewhere else to solve our problems.
  • Use technology in ways that fit our realities.
  • Turn local problems into opportunities for innovation.

Innovation can be global in ambition and local in application.
