The problem

A scan captures a contact.
It doesn't capture the relationship.

Contacts without context

A badge or business card tells us who someone is — not why the conversation mattered.

Leads trapped in silos

Event interactions can end up as disconnected spreadsheets, notes or individual contacts.

Follow-up loses momentum

Without qualification and next-step context, valuable event conversations are harder to act on.

The real value of an event lead isn't the contact record. It's the context behind the connection.
