01 The Problem
The information exists

But understanding it shouldn't require an insider.

Lagos publishes budgets, project information, and performance reports. The challenge is turning those documents into information ordinary citizens can actually understand and use.

Complex public documents

Budgets and reports can be long, technical, and difficult to navigate.

AI without evidence

A fluent AI answer is not automatically a verified answer.

The trust gap

Citizens need information they can understand and independently verify.

“Public information shouldn't require a government insider to understand.”

02 The Solution
Civic information, redesigned

Ask a question. Get the evidence.

CivicPulse turns official Lagos public data into understandable, evidence-backed answers.

01

Ask

Ask natural-language questions about Lagos budgets, spending, and projects.

02

Verify

03

Act

See the source, reporting period, page, and official document so you can check it yourself.

AI explains evidence. It does not invent evidence.
